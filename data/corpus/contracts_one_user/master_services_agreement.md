# Master Services Agreement

## Term and Renewal

The initial term of the agreement is 12 months from the effective date and renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives at least 30 days' written notice before the renewal date.

## Payment Terms

Invoices are due net 30 from the invoice date. Late payments may incur interest at 1 percent per month or the maximum amount permitted by law, whichever is lower.

## Data Security

Vendor will maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect customer data from unauthorized access, use, and disclosure.
