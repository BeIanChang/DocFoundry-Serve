# Benefits FAQ

## Vacation Carryover

Q: Can I roll over unused vacation into next year?

A: Not automatically. Most employees are expected to use accrued vacation by the end of the calendar year. Limited carryover may be approved through the normal process.

Q: How many days can my manager approve without HR?

A: Your manager may approve up to 5 unused days for carryover if the request is submitted before year end.

Q: When does HR get involved?

A: HR reviews requests above 5 days and late requests that may qualify for an exception.

Q: What happens if my request is approved?

A: Approved carryover usually must be used by March 31 of the following year.

Q: What if I forgot to submit the request before the deadline?

A: Late requests are normally denied, but HR may review them if there was a documented emergency, system outage, or company-directed restriction that prevented timely submission.
