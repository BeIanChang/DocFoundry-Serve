# HR Policy Exceptions Memo

## PTO Carryover Exceptions

This memo clarifies when exceptions to the standard PTO carryover rule may be approved.

Manager approval alone may authorize carryover of up to 5 unused vacation days when the request is submitted before December 31 and the employee remains in good standing.

Requests above 5 days require both manager approval and HR approval. HR review is appropriate only when the employee could not reasonably take leave because of documented business continuity needs, a formal leave coordination issue, or an organization-wide restriction on time off.

Approved exceptions must document the reason for the exception, the affected dates, the approving manager, and HR acknowledgment when applicable.

If a request is submitted after the deadline, the default outcome is denial. HR may review late requests only when the delay was caused by a system outage, emergency leave event, or a documented instruction from company leadership to defer time off.

Employees may not stack multiple informal approvals to exceed the 5-day manager-only threshold. Once the request exceeds 5 days, HR approval is required for the entire carryover amount.
