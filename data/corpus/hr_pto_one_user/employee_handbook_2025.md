# Employee Handbook 2025

## Section 4.2 Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue paid vacation throughout the calendar year and are expected to plan time off with their manager in advance. Vacation balances should be used within the year in which they are accrued whenever reasonably possible.

Unused vacation days do not automatically carry over into the next calendar year. Employees are expected to schedule and use accrued vacation by December 31.

Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried over only with written manager approval submitted before year end. Carryover requests submitted after December 31 are generally not approved unless a separately documented exception applies.

Carryover requests above 5 days require Human Resources approval and are limited to exceptional business continuity situations, such as major incidents, staffing shortages during critical launches, or company-directed blackout periods.

Any approved carryover must be used by March 31 of the following year unless Human Resources grants a different deadline in writing.

## Section 4.3 Manager Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for reviewing vacation plans, confirming team coverage, and approving or declining carryover requests in writing. Written approval must identify the number of days approved for carryover and the operational reason supporting the request.

## Section 4.4 Employee Responsibilities

Employees requesting carryover must submit the request through the HR portal and include the number of unused days, the requested carryover amount, and a short explanation. Employees should not assume carryover is approved until written confirmation is received.
